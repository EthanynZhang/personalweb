This project examines how feedback style becomes a pedagogical persona in embodied conversational agents for L2 speaking practice. It compares three agent roles: a Direct Correction Agent, a Gentle Reformulation Agent, and a Guided Support Agent. By studying their effects on willingness to communicate, speaking performance, learner agency, and affective experience, the project treats an agent's persona as an instructional design variable rather than a cosmetic feature. The goal is to understand how embodied AI can balance linguistic improvement with supportive and learner-sensitive interaction.
