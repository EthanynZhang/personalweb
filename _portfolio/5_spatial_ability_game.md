Spatial Ability Game is a Unity-based educational game developed to support and assess children's spatial reasoning through interactive three-dimensional construction tasks. Players manipulate cubes, reproduce target structures, and solve progressively challenging spatial problems while the system records their performance and strategy use. Developed as part of a National Natural Science Foundation of China research project, the game explores how adaptive difficulty and embedded assessment can make spatial visualization skills observable, measurable, and trainable. I served as the project's main game developer.
