EmboRepair is a Social VR system designed to help second-language learners recover from communication breakdowns through embodied and context-sensitive support. When learners hesitate, fall silent, avoid eye contact, or struggle to express an idea, the system can provide low-disruption cues through pointing, shared attention, object highlighting, spatial repositioning, and peer-mediated assistance. The project investigates how these multimodal forms of scaffolding can sustain willingness to communicate while allowing learners to remain active participants in repairing the interaction.
