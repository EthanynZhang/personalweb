Agentic AI-Assisted Debate System is an embodied learning environment that uses AI agents to demonstrate argument structure, diagnose learner performance, and provide adaptive coaching during L2 debate practice. The project compares learner-led, shared-control, and AI-led support to examine how decisions about when feedback begins, what it targets, and when it ends redistribute epistemic work between learners and AI. Its broader goal is to design agentic tutoring that strengthens argumentation and metacognitive regulation without weakening learner agency or independent strategy use.
